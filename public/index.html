How to play
  1. Make a lobby and send your friends the 5-number code (or the invite link).
  2. Every race, 4 to 6 horses show up with silly names (or 2 to 8, if the host picks a number). Pick one and bet 1 to 20 chips. Lots of people can back the same horse, but only the first person to pick it gets to rename it.
  3. The track has 20 gates, or anywhere from 3 to 100 if the host changes it (try a quick 7-gate race). Horses don't run: each one hops forward one gate at a time, at random, so even a horse way out in front can still lose. Every time all the horses make it through the next gate, the race freezes: one horse gets boosted forward a gate ⚡ and another gets set back a gate 🐌. No horse can get more boosts, or more setbacks, than 30% of the gates (rounded up): 6 each on 20 gates, 3 on 7 gates, 30 on 100.
  4. If your horse wins, you win the chips you bet, and you get twice your bet in penalties to dish out to anyone else, split however you like. They get a popup saying you attacked them.
  5. If your horse loses, you take penalties equal to your bet.
  6. Everyone's chips won and penalties taken are tallied under their name in the lobby.
